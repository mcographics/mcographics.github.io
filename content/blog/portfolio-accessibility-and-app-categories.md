The Majestic Creations website has received a set of practical improvements focused on readability, navigation, and a clearer picture of the work in the portfolio. These changes are not a redesign. They extend the existing visual identity while giving visitors more control over how the site is presented.

## Accessibility preferences across the site

A new **Accessibility** menu is available from the shared header. Its settings apply throughout the portfolio, Journal, Community, and About Me pages.

Visitors can now choose:

- **Large or extra-large text**, which scales the complete page rather than isolated paragraphs.
- **High contrast**, with stronger foregrounds, backgrounds, controls, and card boundaries.
- **Color-vision-friendly cues**, using blue and amber accents alongside labels and shapes so meaning does not depend on red and green alone.
- **Underlined links**, making interactive text identifiable without relying only on color.
- **Reduced motion**, which suppresses decorative animation, smooth scrolling, and transitions.

Preferences are stored locally in the visitor's browser. There is no account, cloud profile, or tracking requirement, and a reset control restores the original presentation at any time.

The site also includes a keyboard-accessible skip link, clearer focus indicators, labelled controls, and switch buttons that work with a mouse, touch, Space, or Enter. The controls were revised after the first implementation exposed an interaction problem: decorative switch elements could intercept clicks. Replacing them with real switch buttons made the behavior direct and dependable.

## A clearer Apps filter

The portfolio has always covered more than one creative discipline, but many projects belong to more than one category. A creative tool can still be an application. A faith-based reader can still be an application. An experimental interface can still be an application.

The **Apps** filter now reflects that overlap. Projects retain their original category and can also appear under Apps:

- Creative projects display classifications such as **Creative · Apps**.
- Faith-oriented software displays **Faith-Based · Apps**.
- Application experiments can display **Experiments · Apps**.
- Projects already categorized as Apps remain Apps without a duplicate label.

There are two intentional exceptions. **TanyaOS** remains in Experiments, and **BridgeForge** remains in Game Dev. Every other current portfolio card is included in the Apps view.

## Small changes, better orientation

Accessibility and categorization solve different problems, but both serve the same purpose: helping visitors understand and explore the work without unnecessary friction. One gives people control over presentation; the other makes the portfolio's structure match what the projects actually are.

The website will continue to evolve alongside the applications it documents. Future updates will follow the same principle—preserve the character of Majestic Creations while making the experience clearer, more usable, and more honest about the work.
