There are projects you build on your own, and there are projects that invite you to contribute to a larger vision. **Journey with Jesus** belongs to the second category, and I'm pleased to give it a dedicated place in the Majestic Creations portfolio.

Founded by **Eric Oberhausen**, Journey with Jesus is a Christian animation initiative focused on sharing the Gospel through cinematic, anime-inspired storytelling. The creative vision centers on the life, teachings, miracles, sacrifice, and resurrection of Jesus Christ, with faith, hope, love, and redemption at its heart.

## My role: Editor / Webmaster

My contribution is on the editorial and website side of the project. As **Editor / Webmaster**, I help support the project's online presence—editing website content, maintaining its presentation, and developing the experience visitors use to explore its stories and people.

This is a collaboration, not a claim that Journey with Jesus is one of my independent creations. Eric is the founder, and the animated storytelling mission is the reason the work exists. My focus is helping that vision find a clear, welcoming home on the web.

## A foundation for stories still to come

The website already has a responsive public home, a media-library framework, a **Meet the Characters** section, a **Meet the Team** section, and a store page in preparation. Behind the public experience, an administrative Studio provides tools for managing media, profiles, and discussion as those services are configured and content is approved.

These are foundations, not a promise that finished animated episodes or merchandise are already available. The public media catalog begins empty until completed stories are published. The goal is to make room for that growth without presenting work-in-progress as a finished release.

## A new place for collaborative work

I've added a separate **Collaborations** section to the Majestic Creations website, with Journey with Jesus as the first featured project. This distinction matters: my independent apps and experiments remain in Projects; shared creative work can now receive its own context and proper credit.

The new [Journey with Jesus collaboration page](/collaborations/journey-with-jesus/) introduces the mission, the team roles, and the digital foundation. Its gallery areas are deliberately left ready for approved character artwork, production images, and interface captures that I'll add as the visual story takes shape.

You can [explore the current Journey with Jesus website](https://mcographics.github.io/journey-with-jesus/) and [see the project's repository](https://github.com/mcographics/journey-with-jesus).

This is an early chapter of a meaningful project, and I'm glad to be part of the work behind it.
